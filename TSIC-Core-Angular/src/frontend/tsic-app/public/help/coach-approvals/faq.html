Coach Approvals

A requested team is not access — approval is.

Coaches sit in this queue with pending requests until you grant them. Nothing they asked for takes effect on its own.

How do I actually approve a coach for a team?

In the coach's Teams cell, check the team they requested — that grant is immediate. Use Grant all to approve every team they asked for at once. To put them on a team they didn't request, use Add team.

What's the difference between a requested team and an assigned team?

A request is what the coach asked for (marked as coming from the coach); it's still pending. An assigned team is one they currently hold because you granted it. The Teams checklist shows granted teams on top and pending requests below.

How do I remove one team without removing the coach?

Uncheck just that team in the Teams cell — it removes only that grant (after a quick confirm). The removed team stays listed in the cell, so you can re-grant it later — and the row becomes selectable for the batch grant buttons again.

How do I approve several coaches at once?

Check the selection box on the right of each row, then click Grant Requests of Checked Rows Only above the grid (it appears once something is checked) — it grants every ungranted team on those rows in one pass. The header's Select All With Requests checks every listed registrant with something to grant, and it respects the status chips and search, so filter first and you're selecting exactly who you see. Rows with nothing to grant have a muted checkbox — there's nothing to batch for them. A confirmation dialog shows the registrant and team counts before anything is written, and if one grant fails the rest still complete; the failed rows stay checked for a retry.

Why don't I see a "Grant All Requests" button?

Two possible reasons. If nothing in the current list has an ungranted team, the batch controls don't render at all — there's nothing for them to do. Beyond that, the button only appears when coaches & staff cannot view team rosters on this event — the "Allow coaches & staff to view their team roster" setting under Job Settings → Teams/ClubReps → Roster Visibility. With roster viewing on, every grant hands the coach the personal information of the players on those teams, so blanket granting is deliberately not offered — vet, check rows, and use Grant Requests of Checked Rows Only instead.

How do I deny a coach — someone who shouldn't be on any team?

Just don't grant them anything — and uncheck any team they already hold. A registrant with no granted teams has no roles on this event: no roster access, no staff messaging, nothing. There's no separate deny or deactivate button because there's nothing left to turn off; they simply stay listed under Unassigned.

How do I know a coach is who they say they are?

Two signals. Coached before shows their prior team/job history so you can recognize a returning coach. On lacrosse events, the Verified badge shows whether they proved ownership of their USA Lacrosse membership email at sign-up, with the membership number and expiry; Re-validate refreshes that check.

The status chips at the top — what are they for?

They filter the list by Unassigned vs Assigned and carry live counts, so they double as a progress meter — how many coaches still need at least one team. Picking a chip pins the visible set; granting a coach updates the counts but leaves the row in place until you re-pick a chip or refresh.

Where do I move players and coaches between teams?

That's the Roster Swapper, which this queue is part of. This screen is specifically the approval gate for coach requests; the Swapper handles moving people between existing teams.

Tip: use the Unassigned chip as your worklist — it's every coach still waiting on a team. When that count reaches zero, everyone who registered has been reviewed.
