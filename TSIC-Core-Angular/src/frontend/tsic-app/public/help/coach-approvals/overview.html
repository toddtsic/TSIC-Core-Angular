Coach Approvals

This is the gate between "a coach signed up" and "a coach is on a team." Coaches register and request the teams they want to coach — but those requests do nothing until you approve them here. Each row is one coach; you decide which teams they actually get.

Granting a team gives a real adult access to that team.

Rosters, contact info, team messaging — approval is what unlocks it. Vet before you grant.

Reading a coach row

  • Status — the row's color shows whether a coach is Unassigned (holds no teams) or Assigned (holds at least one). The chips at the top filter by status and double as a live progress meter.
  • Coached before — a popover of the coach's prior team/job history, so you can recognize a returning coach.
  • Verified — for lacrosse events, shows whether the coach proved ownership of their USA Lacrosse membership email at sign-up, along with their membership number and expiry. Use Re-validate to refresh it.

Granting and removing teams

The Teams cell is the one control that does everything — it's a checklist:

  • Check a requested team to grant it. Grant all approves every team the coach asked for at once.
  • Add team places the coach on a team they didn't request — useful when you're assigning coaches yourself.
  • Uncheck a granted team to remove that one grant (it asks you to confirm).

Acting on a coach recolors the row in place — it won't jump or disappear under your cursor, so you can work down the list steadily.

Approving in bulk

For a long queue you don't have to work one row at a time. Each row has a selection checkbox on the right, and the buttons above that column act on it:

  • Approve Checked grants every pending request for the coaches you've checked. Checking named coaches is still per-coach vetting — just batched.
  • Select All Unassigned, in the column header, checks every listed coach who has pending requests. It follows the status chips and the search box, so it only ever selects coaches you can see.
  • Approve All Pending grants every pending request in the current list. This button only appears when coaches & staff cannot view team rosters on this event (Job Settings → Teams/ClubReps → Roster Visibility) — because then an approval exposes no player personal information. When roster viewing is on, approval hands over player contact details, so it stays a coach-by-coach decision.

The counts on both buttons are pending-only: teams already granted are never touched or re-sent, and a checked coach with nothing outstanding is skipped (the confirmation says so). A confirmation dialog spells out exactly what the approval grants on this event before anything is written. If one approval fails, the rest of the batch still completes — the failed coaches stay checked so you can retry.

Deactivating a coach

Deactivate, at the bottom of a coach's Teams cell, is the coach-level deny: it removes all their teams and drops them from the queue. It asks you to confirm first, because it's the strongest action on this screen.

Common mistakes

  • Granting without vetting. Check "Coached before" and, on lacrosse events, the Verified badge before approving someone you don't recognize.
  • Confusing "request" with "granted." A team the coach asked for is still just a request until you check it. Pending requests aren't access.
  • Deactivating when you meant to remove one team. Deactivate drops the whole coach; uncheck a single team in the Teams cell to remove just that one.
