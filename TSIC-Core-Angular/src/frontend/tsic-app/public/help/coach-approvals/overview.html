Coach Approvals

This is the gate between "a coach signed up" and "a coach is on a team." Coaches register and request the teams they want to coach — but those requests do nothing until you approve them here. Each row is one coach; you decide which teams they actually get.

Granting a team gives a real adult access to that team.

Rosters, contact info, team messaging — approval is what unlocks it. Vet before you grant.

Reading a coach row

  • Status — the row's color shows whether a coach is Unassigned (holds no teams) or Assigned (holds at least one). The chips at the top filter by status and double as a live progress meter.
  • Coached before — a popover of the coach's prior team/job history, so you can recognize a returning coach.
  • Verified — for lacrosse events, shows whether the coach proved ownership of their USA Lacrosse membership email at sign-up, along with their membership number and expiry. Use Re-validate to refresh it.

Granting and removing teams

The Teams cell is the one control that does everything — it's a checklist:

  • Check a requested team to grant it. Grant all approves every team the coach asked for at once.
  • Add team places the coach on a team they didn't request — useful when you're assigning coaches yourself.
  • Uncheck a granted team to remove that one grant (it asks you to confirm).

Acting on a coach recolors the row in place — it won't jump or disappear under your cursor, so you can work down the list steadily.

Granting in bulk

For a long queue you don't have to work one row at a time. Each row has a selection checkbox on the right, and the buttons above that column act on it. A row is selectable whenever it has a recorded team that isn't currently granted — a team the coach requested, or one you removed earlier. Rows with nothing to grant have a muted checkbox, and the batch controls only appear when they have something to act on:

  • Grant Requests of Checked Rows Only appears once you've checked at least one row, and grants every ungranted team on those rows. Checking named registrants is still per-coach vetting — just batched.
  • Select All With Requests, in the column header, checks every listed registrant with something to grant. It follows the status chips and the search box, so it only ever selects rows you can see.
  • Grant All Requests grants every ungranted team in the current list. This button only appears when coaches & staff cannot view team rosters on this event (Job Settings → Teams/ClubReps → Roster Visibility) — because then a grant exposes no player personal information. When roster viewing is on, a grant hands over player contact details, so it stays a coach-by-coach decision.

The counts on both buttons cover only what is ungranted: teams already granted are never touched or re-sent. A confirmation dialog spells out exactly what the grant unlocks on this event before anything is written. If one grant fails, the rest of the batch still completes — the failed rows stay checked so you can retry.

Declining a coach

There is no separate deny button — unchecking is the whole story. A registrant holding no teams has no roles on this event: they can't see rosters, can't be messaged as staff, and simply remain listed as Unassigned. To decline someone, remove (or never grant) their teams and move on.

Common mistakes

  • Granting without vetting. Check "Coached before" and, on lacrosse events, the Verified badge before approving someone you don't recognize.
  • Confusing "request" with "granted." A team the coach asked for is still just a request until you check it. Pending requests aren't access.
  • Looking for a delete button. Declining a coach isn't an action — a coach with no granted teams already has no access. Unchecking their teams is all it takes.
