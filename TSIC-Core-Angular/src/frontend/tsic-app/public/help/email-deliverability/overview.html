Check My Email

Wondering why you haven't received emails from your program? This page shows whether our email service can deliver to the addresses on your account, lets you fix a blocked address yourself, and lists the emails already sent to you — so you can see exactly what went out and when.

Current Status tab

  • Every email address on your account is checked and shown with a status pill: OK (we can deliver), Blocked (our email service has stopped sending to it), or Unknown.
  • A blocked address shows when and why it was blocked — usually a bounced message or a spam report from your mail provider.
  • Blocked? Click the fix button to remove the block. Delivery resumes immediately.
  • Use Send test to have a real test message sent to an address — then check your inbox (and spam folder) to confirm it arrives.
Blocked addresses hear nothing at all.

When an address is blocked, messages aren't delayed — they're skipped entirely, with no bounce or error. If you've had total silence from your program, this is the first thing to check.

E-Mail History tab

  • A list of the emails this program has sent to your addresses — subject, recipient, and date.
  • Open a row to read the actual message that was sent.
  • If a message appears here but never reached your inbox, look in your spam/junk folder and add the sender to your contacts.

If mail still doesn't arrive

Status is OK, the test send succeeded, and you've checked spam — but still nothing? Contact support@teamsportsinfo.com and mention what this page showed; that detail speeds things up considerably.
