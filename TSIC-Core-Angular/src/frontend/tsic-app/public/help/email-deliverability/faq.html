Check My Email

This page covers your own account's addresses.

The addresses checked here come from your family and player profile. To check a different address, add it to your profile first.

Why was my address blocked in the first place?

The two usual causes: a message to you bounced (your mailbox was full, or the address had a typo or didn't exist at the time), or a message was reported as spam from your mailbox — sometimes by an overzealous filter rather than a person. Either way, the email service protects itself by pausing all delivery to that address until the block is removed.

I removed the block — will I get the emails I missed?

No — messages skipped while blocked aren't re-sent automatically. Check the E-Mail History tab to read anything you missed; new messages will arrive normally from now on.

The test send says it succeeded but nothing arrived.

That means our service dispatched it and your provider accepted it — so it's landing somewhere in your mailbox. Check spam/junk and promotions, search for "teamsportsinfo", and add the sender to your contacts so future mail goes to your inbox.

Why is the Send Test button briefly disabled after I use it?

There's a short cooldown between test sends to each address. Wait a minute and it re-enables — one test at a time is all a diagnosis needs anyway.

The history doesn't show an email my program says they sent.

This page shows emails sent to your addresses for this program. If the message went to a different family member's address, sign in as that member — or ask your program director, who can see the full send log for the event.

Tip: adding the sender address to your contacts is the single most effective way to keep program emails out of spam — mail providers rarely filter a known contact.
