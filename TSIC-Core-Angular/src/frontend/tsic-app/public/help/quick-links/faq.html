How do I set Quick Links for Player and Coach Self-Rostering?

Self-rostering means the team registers first, then players and coaches attach themselves to a team that already exists. So the order matters — turn these on in sequence:

  1. Make sure teams exist. Both paths attach a person to a team, so with no teams there is nothing to join. Either add teams yourself, or run Register Team first and let club reps create them.
  2. Turn on Allow Player Self-Rostering.
  3. Turn on Allow Coach Registration.
  4. On tournament events, turn on Rosters (public access) so club reps, coaches, directors, recruiters and families can see who's on each team. Only a player's name, position and uniform number are displayed — nothing else. Coaches are listed on the rosters as well.

Switched on but nothing appears on the public page?

Coach registration warns you when no teams exist yet — the switch still saves, but the "Register Coach" Smart Bulletin stays hidden until teams are added. Add the teams and it appears on its own; you don't need to re-flip anything.

On a league event the same switch reads Allow Player Registration rather than Self-Rostering, but it does the same thing — leagues don't register teams, so players always self-roster onto the teams you've built.

What do these switches actually do?

Each one shows or hides a Smart Bulletin on your event's public landing page — player registration, team registration, coach sign-up, store, and the like. On makes it appear to visitors; off retires it.

Do I need to save after flipping a switch?

No — each toggle saves on its own the instant you flip it, and the change is live on the public page right away. There's no separate Save button, so treat every flip as immediate.

Why is one of the switches greyed out?

A switch with a "SuperUser only" lock badge is TSIC-managed (insurance, store) — the state shown is real, but changing it is ours; contact us. Switches that don't apply to your event type at all simply don't appear on the list. (Rows also disable briefly while a flip is saving.)

A switch showed a warning when I turned it on. Did it fail?

No — the warning is a heads-up about a consequence (like opening coach registration before any teams exist), not a block. If that's intentional, leave it on; the switch still saved.

Are these the same settings as in Configure Job?

Yes. The same flags live in their proper Configure Job tabs; this screen just collects the public-facing ones in one place for speed. Change it here or there — it's the same setting.
