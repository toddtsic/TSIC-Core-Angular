Info Smart Bulletins — no switch of their own

Not every Smart Bulletin has a row on this page. The info Smart Bulletins assemble themselves from your event's setup and ride along with the switches you do have. The best example — the USA Lacrosse membership notice:

BEFORE YOU REGISTER

USA Lacrosse Membership Required

  • Name & date of birth match. Family Account details must match USA Lacrosse records exactly.
  • Membership stays active. Must not expire before December 31, 2025.
  • Age verification complete. USA Lacrosse's one-time age verification.

It appears on its own when two things are true: player registration is open (your switch), and the player profile requires a USA Lacrosse membership number (your registration setup). The deadline it quotes is your configured validation window — change the setup and the Smart Bulletin rewrites itself; close registration and it retires with the registration Smart Bulletin.

That's the pattern for info Smart Bulletins generally: they exist to answer a family's question before it becomes your support call, and their content is derived from configuration — you never author or maintain them.

Switch ON but no Smart Bulletin? Five clauses decide

Your switch is one vote of five. The public "Register Player" Smart Bulletin only shows when all of these pass:

  • The event isn't over
  • No newer edition of this event has replaced it
  • The registration switch is on (this page)
  • Fees are configured
  • A team is open for players to join

One ✗ anywhere and the Smart Bulletin stays hidden — with your switch honestly ON. The same is true for warnings you'll see right here on some rows: coach registration with no teams built yet, View Schedule with no games entered. The Smart Bulletin appears by itself the moment the missing fact exists.

Tip: the live version of this checklist — with real dates, counts, and a fix link per failing line — sits on the Configure Job registration tabs. It's the server's own evaluation, so it can't disagree with what the public page is doing.

Reading the rows

  • The tip line states the current truth. Under every switch, the small line rewords itself for the position the switch is in — ON: "Teams can register — the 'Register Team' Smart Bulletin shows on the landing page." OFF: "Team registration is closed — the Smart Bulletin is hidden." Read the line, not just the switch.
  • Locked switches are TSIC-managed. RegSaver insurance (player and team) and the Store carry a SuperUser only badge — the state shown is real; the flip just isn't yours. Ask us to change them.
  • Labels adapt to your event type. A tournament registers teams and players join one, so its switch reads "Allow Player Self-Rostering" where a league's reads "Allow Player Registration." Switches that can't apply to your event type simply don't appear — a shorter list means those actions aren't part of this event's shape.

Common mistakes

  • Registration "isn't working" — but the switch is off. If families can't register, check that its toggle is on here first. This is the most common cause.
  • Leaving a path open too long. A switch left on keeps accepting sign-ups; turn it off when a registration window closes.
  • Expecting a locked switch to flip. The lock badge means the setting is TSIC-managed for your event — contact us rather than clicking harder.
