US Lax Rankings

This tool pulls published national club lacrosse rankings and attaches them to your registered teams, so you can build pools by team strength rather than by guesswork. It does the heavy lifting by auto-matching ranking entries to your teams; you review, fix the ones it wasn't sure about, and save. Saved rankings appear as Nat'l Rank in Pool Assignment.

This is an experimental tool.

The rankings are read live from a third-party website (usclublax.com) that we do not control. If they change their page layout, rename an age group, or go offline, results here can change or stop working without notice. Treat it as an aid to seeding, not a source of record — check the results before you rely on them, and use at your own discretion.

Who can use it

Browsing the published rankings works on any event — pick a season and an age group and the table appears. Matching those rankings to your registered teams is available on tournament events only, because seeding pools is what it exists for. On a non-tournament event the matching controls simply aren't shown.

The workflow

  1. Pick a season. The ranking site publishes one season at a time, and the Season list mirrors what they currently offer — it defaults to the newest. When you're seeding a new event, choose the most recently completed season instead; it has a full year of results behind it. Note that the available age groups differ from season to season, so the age group list reloads when you switch.
  2. Pick two age groups. Choose the published ranking group, then the registered age group in your event it corresponds to. Published groups are named by graduation class — "Girls 2029" means the class of 2029 — so pair each with the age group holding those players. It auto-aligns as soon as both are chosen.
  3. Review the matches. Each of your teams shows the ranking it was paired with and a confidence — high, medium, or low — based on how closely the names matched.
  4. Fix the uncertain ones. For a team that didn't match (or matched wrong), click it, then pick the correct ranking from the sidebar. You can also undo a match to send it back.
  5. Save. Choose a threshold — high-confidence only, high + medium, or everything — and the rankings are written onto those teams. Manual matches you made are always saved too.
Confidence tells you how much to trust an auto-match.

Teams are paired by comparing club name, colour, graduation year and state. High = the names lined up cleanly. Medium = probably right, worth a glance. Low / unmatched = check it yourself before saving. Start by saving high-confidence, then work through the rest by hand.

Other actions

  • Browse without matching. Choose a season and a ranking age group and leave your event's age group unset — the published table appears on its own, with filter and sort, so you can look up where a club sits before you commit to anything.
  • Filter the table to unmatched, high, or medium to focus your review.
  • Export CSV to get the alignment as a spreadsheet.
  • Clear rankings removes saved rankings from the selected age group's teams — a clean slate if an import went wrong.

Common mistakes

  • Saving everything blindly. "All" includes low-confidence guesses. Review low/unmatched first, or you'll pin the wrong ranking on a team.
  • Mismatched age groups. Aligning a U15 national list against your U13 teams produces nonsense matches — pair like with like, and remember the published groups are by graduation class.
  • Seeding off a season that has barely started. A season with few games played has thin ratings. Switch to the last completed season instead.
  • Forgetting to save. Reviewing and matching on screen isn't persisted until you Save; leave without saving and the work is lost.
