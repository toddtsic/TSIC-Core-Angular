Scheduling Checklist

Work the steps top to bottom.

Each step links to the screen that turns it green. When the required steps are green, Build unlocks.

Why is Build Schedule locked?

One or more of the steps above it isn't finished — the lock row names which ones. The most common blocker is teams still sitting in the Unassigned pool: those teams can never be placed in a schedule, so the build stays locked until every age group is fully pooled.

What does the detail line under a step mean?

It's the live list of what's missing — for example "2029: 12 of 59" under Pools means the 2029 age group still has 12 unpooled teams, and "No dates for: 2030" means that age group has no play dates yet. Fix exactly what's named and the step turns green.

Pairings shows missing pool sizes. Do I have to fix that?

No — pairings are informational. Any missing pool size is generated automatically when the build runs. Visit the Pairings screen only if you want to review or customize who-plays-whom beforehand.

Why are the Tools grayed out?

The Tools — View Schedule, Master Schedule, Rescheduler, QA Results and friends — operate on games, so they unlock once the schedule has been built. Until then the checklist steps are the whole job.

Tip: for a returning event, the checklist is the fastest way to confirm last year's setup carried forward — a quick glance shows whether dates, fields, and guarantees are already green before you build.
