Referee Calendar

Calendar reads, Assignment writes.

This page never changes an assignment — it shows and exports what the Referee Assignment page has decided.

How do I get one referee's personal schedule?

Pick them in the Referee filter — every view and export now covers just their games. That filtered export is exactly what you send an official before the event.

What counts as a "Solo" assignment, and is it a problem?

A game with exactly one official assigned. Whether that's a problem depends on your event — younger divisions often run one official by design, while championship games usually expect a crew. The chip is a prompt to look, not an error.

An assignment I just made isn't showing.

Click the refresh button in the header — the calendar loads its snapshot when you open it and doesn't poll. If it's still missing after a refresh, confirm the assignment was actually saved on the Referee Assignment page.

Can a referee see this themselves?

Referee assignors see this page from their own menu. Individual officials generally get their schedule from the export you send them — filter to their name and export.

Agenda or Grid — which should I use?

Agenda for time-ordered reading (day-of operations, spotting back-to-backs at different fields); Grid for scanning coverage across many games at once. Same data, two shapes.

Tip: before publishing a schedule change, filter by each affected referee and eyeball their agenda for impossible transitions — two games at overlapping times on distant fields is the classic one.
