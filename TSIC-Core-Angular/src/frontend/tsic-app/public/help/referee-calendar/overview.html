Referee Calendar

The read-and-export view of every referee assignment in the event. Where Referee Assignment is the workbench for making and changing assignments, the calendar answers the schedule questions: who works when, where, and with whom.

Both referee screens live under Officials in the menu — separate from Scheduling, because officiating is its own job with its own role. Referee assignors see only these two.

The stats bar

  • Assignments — total referee-to-game assignments in the event.
  • Referees — how many distinct officials are working.
  • Solo — games covered by a single official. This chip appears only when solo games exist; treat it as a to-do count if your event expects crews.

Views and filtering

  • Agenda — assignments in time order, the natural view for a day-of run sheet.
  • Grid — the tabular layout for scanning across games and crews.
  • The Referee filter narrows everything to one official — their personal schedule, ready to check for gaps or send along.
Filter first, then export.

Exports respect the current filter — select one referee and export to hand each official their own schedule, or leave the filter on All for the master sheet.

What this page doesn't do

It's deliberately read-only. To add, move, or remove an assignment, use Referee Assignment — changes made there appear here on the next refresh (the circular-arrow button up top).
