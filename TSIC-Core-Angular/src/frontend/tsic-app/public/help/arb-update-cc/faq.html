Update Payment Method — questions

I missed an installment — how do I pay it?

Right here. There's no separate screen for a missed payment: the Balance Due at the top of this page is the past-due amount, and Update Card & Pay Balance charges it while storing your new card.

  • Balance Due 0.00 — you're up to date. Nothing is charged now; the card is saved for future installments.
  • Any other amount — that's what the button will charge today.

You can enter the same card you already had on file if it's working again — the charge goes through either way. Your installment schedule and amounts don't change.

This page is for a plan that's still running.

If your automatic payments have been cancelled or have come to an end, there's no card on file left to update. Use Pay Balance Due in the User Menu instead — it takes a card and settles what's owed in a single payment.

I have more than one player. Does this cover all of them?

No — each player's automatic-payment plan holds its own card, so you'll need to repeat this for each one. Open the User Menu in the upper right, choose Switch Role, select your next player, and come back to Update CC Info.

Before entering card details, check that the player name and event shown at the top of this page are the ones you meant to pay for.

Will this charge my card right now?

Yes — updating the card also pays the Balance Due shown at the top. If that figure is zero, no charge is made; the page just stores your new card for future drafts.

Does this change my payment schedule or amounts?

No. It only swaps the card on file and settles today's balance. The plan's timing and installment amounts stay exactly as they were.

Why is the Update button greyed out?

Every field is required. Fill in all of the cardholder and card details — name, address, ZIP, email, card number, CVV, and expiration month and year — and the button becomes active.

The page says it couldn't load my subscription.

The link may be invalid or expired, or it's missing its registration reference. Open it again from the Update CC Info item in your User Menu, or contact the event director for a fresh link.

The update failed — what now?

You'll see the reason on screen (for example, the card was declined). Double-check the card number, expiration, and CVV, then try again — or use a different card.

If the card is definitely good and the page still won't complete, it's usually the device or network:

  • Try a different browser.
  • Try a different device — if you're on a phone, try a computer.
  • Avoid school or work devices; their firewalls frequently block payment forms.
How do I get a receipt?

Enter the email you'd like the receipt sent to in the cardholder section. On success the page also shows the amount charged and a transaction reference for your records.
