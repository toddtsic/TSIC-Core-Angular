Customer Job Revenue

Revenue across your jobs, in one place. Pick a date range, optionally narrow to specific jobs, and see what each job collected — with registration counts and admin-fee detail alongside, so the money always has its context.

Running a report

  • Choose a Start and End month, and (optionally) select individual jobs in the Filter Jobs box — leave it empty to include everything.
  • Click Submit. The report doesn't auto-refresh when you change filters; Submit runs it.

The three tabs

  • Revenue Rollup — the headline numbers: collected revenue summarized per job for the selected window.
  • Player/Team Counts — the volume behind the money: how many registrations produced those totals, so you can tell a price change from a participation change.
  • Admin Fee Records — the fee-level detail behind the rollup, for reconciling what was charged where.
Same window, three angles.

All tabs show the same date range and job selection — switch tabs to change the lens, not the data.

Reading it well

  • Comparing seasons? Run the same month span for each year and compare rollups side by side.
  • A revenue dip with flat counts points at pricing/discounts; a dip with falling counts is participation.
