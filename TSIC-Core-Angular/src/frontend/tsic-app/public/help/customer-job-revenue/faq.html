Changing the dates or job selection doesn't refresh the report — click Submit to run the new query.

Which jobs are included if I leave the job filter empty?

All of your jobs with activity in the selected window. Use the filter only when you want to isolate specific events — say, comparing two tournaments without the leagues mixed in.

Revenue looks lower than I expected for a job.

Check the window first — revenue lands in the month it was collected, and registrations often pay months before the event runs. Widen the date range before concluding anything. Then check the Player/Team Counts tab: if counts are down too, it's participation, not a reporting gap.

What's in Admin Fee Records that the rollup doesn't show?

The individual fee entries behind the summarized totals — what was charged, on which job. When a rollup number looks off, this tab is where you find which records produced it.

Can I get this as a spreadsheet?

The grids support the standard export tooling where offered; for the formal month-end accounting exports (invoices, grand totals, reconciliation), use the dedicated items in the Accounting menu — those are the books of record.

Why don't my directors see this page?

It spans revenue across jobs, so it's limited to SuperDirector and above. Per-job financials for a director live in that job's own reports.

Tip: for year-over-year comparisons, keep the month span identical and change only the year — mismatched windows are the most common cause of "revenue is down" false alarms.
