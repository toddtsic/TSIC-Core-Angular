E-Mail Troubleshooter

Your tool for "I never got your email" reports. It answers the two questions that matter: is this address blocked by the email provider, and if not, whose side is the failure on — ours or the recipient's?

Suppression List tab

  • Enter one or more addresses (separate with semicolons) and click Check Suppression.
  • Each address comes back Suppressed or Not suppressed. Suppressed means the email provider (Amazon SES) has stopped sending to it — usually after a hard bounce or a spam complaint — along with the reason and the date it happened.
  • Click Remove from suppression to un-stick the address. Future sends resume immediately.
Suppression is the #1 cause of "silent" non-delivery.

A suppressed address gets nothing — no bounce notice, no error — the system simply skips it. If someone says they've received nothing for weeks, check here first.

Investigate E-Mail Failure tab

  • Sends a real test message to each address you enter, then reports the outcome.
  • Each address gets a verdict: Recipient side (we dispatched it fine — the problem is their spam folder, mailbox, or provider), Our side (our sending path rejected it), or Inconclusive.
  • Include a known-good control address — your own inbox — alongside the suspect one. If the control arrives and the suspect doesn't, that's a clean recipient-side diagnosis you can relay with confidence.

A practical workflow

  • Parent reports missing email → check Suppression → if suppressed, remove and tell them it's fixed.
  • Not suppressed → run Investigate with their address + your control.
  • Recipient-side verdict → have them check spam/junk and safelist the sender; the system is doing its job.
