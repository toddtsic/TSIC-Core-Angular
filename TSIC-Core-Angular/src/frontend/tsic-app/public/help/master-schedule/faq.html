Master Schedule

This screen shows the schedule — it doesn't edit it.

Build games on the hub's Schedule segment and move individual games in the Rescheduler; use this view to review and export the result.

How do I switch between days?

Use the day tabs across the top of the grid. Each tab shows one day; fields are the columns and times run down the left, so a cell is the game at that field and time.

Can I export just one day instead of the whole event?

Yes. Export Day downloads only the day you're currently viewing; Export Full downloads every day in a single Excel workbook. Both come out as .xlsx files.

The grid is huge and hard to navigate. Is there a faster way?

Press M to open the minimap — a scaled-down map of the whole grid. Click anywhere on it to jump there, and press Esc to close it.

A game is in the wrong slot. How do I fix it from here?

You don't fix it here — this is a read view. To move a single game use the Rescheduler; to rebuild or delete games switch to the Schedule segment. Then come back to Master to confirm and re-export.

A day is missing or the grid is blank. Why?

Master only shows games that have been built and dated. A blank grid or absent day usually means those games haven't been generated yet — check the Configure and Schedule segments, or open the Scheduling Checklist, which names exactly what's still missing.

I opened this from the checklist and landed in the Schedule hub. Is that right?

Yes. Master is one of the hub's four segments, so the checklist's Master Schedule tool opens the hub with Master already selected. The grid, the minimap, and both exports are all exactly the same — you just get the segment control and the build surface a click away.

Tip: run QA before you export a schedule for distribution. The exported workbook is exactly what people will play from, so it's worth confirming there are no conflicts first.
