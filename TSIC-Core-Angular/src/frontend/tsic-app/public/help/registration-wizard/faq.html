Registration

Nothing is charged, and no one is registered, until you confirm payment.

Everything before that step is safe to explore, back out of, and redo.

Is my player registered before I pay?

No. Move Back and Next through the steps as much as you like — the player is registered only once you confirm payment on the final step.

How do I read the progress bar?

A finished step turns green with a check; the step you're on is highlighted:

Player → 2. Details → 3. Waivers → 4. Pay
I closed the browser partway through. Did I lose everything?

No. Progress is saved as you go, so you can return and pick up where you left off. Since nothing is final until payment, an unfinished registration just waits for you.

Why is the "Continue" button greyed out?

A step still needs something — a required field, or a waiver not yet accepted. The step points out what's missing; once it's handled, the button turns active:

Can I go back and fix an earlier step?

Yes — use Back anytime. A green check means the step is complete, not locked. Nothing is committed until you confirm payment.

How do I register more than one player?

Finish this player all the way through, then start the wizard again for the next. Each player is registered separately.

What's the difference between the deposit and the full price?

When the event allows it, the payment step offers a smaller deposit to hold the spot now, with the balance due later. If there's no deposit option, you pay the full amount.

Can I pay in installments instead of all at once?

On events that offer it, the payment step lets you either pay in full at registration or set up automatic payments (an installment plan). If you choose automatic payments, the fee is split into a set number of equal monthly installments:

  • The first payment is charged the day after you register.
  • The remaining payments are charged automatically once a month from that first date, until the balance is paid.

You'll receive a receipt each time a payment is processed. If an event doesn't show an automatic-payment option, it isn't offered for that registration.

How do I update my card — or pay a missed installment — on automatic payments?

While your plan is still running, both are the same screen: updating your card also settles anything past due, so you don't need to pay separately first.

If your automatic payments have already stopped, use "Pay Balance Due" instead.

Once a plan has been cancelled or has come to an end, there's no longer a card on file to update — so the menu offers Pay Balance Due rather than Update CC Info. It takes a card and settles what's owed in one payment.

  1. Sign in — from the upper right of your event's home page, or at teamsportsinfo.com.
  2. Choose your player's role — the one with automatic payments set up.
  3. Open the User Menu in the upper right and select Update CC Info.
  4. Fill in the Update Payment Method form and click Update Card & Pay Balance.
  5. Have more than one player? Use Switch Role in the same menu to select each additional player and repeat — every player's subscription holds its own card.

Check Balance Due at the top of that form before you submit — it tells you what the button will charge:

  • 0.00 — you're up to date. Nothing is charged; the new card is simply stored for future installments.
  • Any other amount — that's a missed installment, and it's charged when you click the button.
Don't see "Update CC Info" in the menu?

It appears only for a player whose automatic payments are currently running. Three reasons it might be missing: this player was never on a plan; the plan has ended or been cancelled (look for Pay Balance Due instead); or you're signed in under a different player — try Switch Role.

If the page won't cooperate:

  • Confirm you're on the right event and the right player before entering card details.
  • Try a different browser, or a different device.
  • On a phone? Try a computer.
  • Avoid school or work devices — their firewalls often block payment forms.
Tip: if a price, date, or required field looks wrong, don't guess — the league contact on the event's landing page would rather answer a quick question than untangle it later.
