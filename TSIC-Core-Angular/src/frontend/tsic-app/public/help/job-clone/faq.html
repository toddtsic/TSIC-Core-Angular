Clone Job

A clone never arrives live.

Registration links closed, bulletins inactive, administrators deactivated — always, regardless of how the source job was set. That's deliberate: you verify the new job first, then turn things on.

Which job gets cloned?

The job you're currently in — its name and path are shown under the page title. To clone a different job, switch into it first, then come back to this screen.

Why didn't my teams come across?

Two possibilities. If you picked Leagues, age groups and pools — no teams, that's by design: clubs re-register their teams each season. If you picked the option with teams, only the house teams your organization created and still owns are copied — teams belonging to outside clubs are never cloned, and neither are competing, waitlisted/dropped, or inactive teams. The Teams line in the Plan panel shows exactly how many clone and how many are excluded, and why.

Where is last season's waitlist?

Waitlists are never cloned. They're generated automatically on the new job, for all job types, whenever an age group's Max number is exceeded — so the new season's waitlist reflects the new season's registrations, not last year's.

The pools all say "Unassigned" on the new job.

You chose Start fresh for pools — named pools were dropped and every age group got a single Unassigned pool, with any copied teams parked in it. That's the intended workflow: re-pool from scratch once registration closes. If you wanted last season's pool names back, they were an option at clone time (Keep last season's pools); on the new job you can simply recreate them on the pool-assignment screen.

The new job's banner still shows last season's dates.

The clone carried the source banner as-is — the Start on a plain banner option wasn't checked. Fix it on the new job under Configure → Job → Branding: upload this season's artwork, or edit the headline and caption. The banner preview on this screen exists precisely so you catch this before cloning.

I cloned to a different customer. Anything special to check?

Two things. Payments on the new job bill to the new customer's merchant account — that's what the cross-customer warning is about. And the header logo carried over from the previous owner and shows on every page — replace it under Configure → Job → Branding before the site goes public.

Is the new site visible to the public before I'm ready?

The new job doesn't appear in the public job listing, but its own pages are reachable at its web address the moment the clone commits. The real protection is the safe state: registration is closed and cloned administrators are inactive, so nobody can sign up or change anything. Finish your setup, then open registration under Configure → Quick Links.

The event-end warning says the job will be "concluded". What does that mean?

A job whose event end date is in the past reads as over, and a concluded job shows no registration links on its public site — whatever toggles you turn on. Shifted forward from an older event, the end date can land in the past without you noticing; set it to this season's actual end date before cloning.

Should I turn on the season roll-forward?

Rolling into a new season — yes: years in names and text advance one, and birth-date/graduation ranges shift with them. Building a second job for the same season — no: there's nothing to advance, and the plan will note the clone stays in the same year.

Why is the Clone button disabled?

Either something on the form is invalid (a path or name collision, an invalid slug — the field shows the error inline), the affirmation checkbox isn't ticked, or the plan is still refreshing. The clone always runs against the plan you're looking at — if you've just changed a field, wait for the Plan header to read current.

Where are my discount codes?

Discount codes are never cloned — last season's codes on a new season's prices is a mistake waiting to happen. Add this season's codes on the new job under Configure → Discount Codes.

I made a practice clone. Can I delete it?

On the development and staging environments, yes — Configure → Job offers a delete for sandbox clones. Production jobs are never deleted.

Tip: read the Plan panel like a receipt before you click Clone. The warnings and the per-step counts are the clone telling you, in advance, everything it's about to do — a surprise on the new job means something was visible there first.
