Schedule Dashboard

This screen reports; it doesn't change anything.

Read what the build produced here, then fix it on the Scheduling Checklist or the Schedule hub.

How do I get to this screen?

Click the stat band under the title on the Scheduling Checklist — the row showing the date span, fields, and divisions. The arrow beside this page's title takes you back.

It says "No games scheduled yet."

The schedule hasn't been built. Everything on this page is a summary of existing games, so there's nothing to show until the build has run. Work the Scheduling Checklist steps first.

A tile is highlighted and says teams are without a game. What now?

The build couldn't place them. The usual causes, in order of likelihood: those teams are still in the Unassigned pool; their pool has no timeslots on a day the age group plays; or there simply weren't enough open slots. The Scheduling Checklist catches the first two by name — start there, then re-build.

Worth knowing: a build that can't place everything doesn't fail, it just places less. These tiles are the honest count of what came up short.

Why are fewer fields in use than I assigned to the event?

Assigning a field to the event only makes it available. It gets games only if it's also ticked for a day in Assign Timeslots. A field with no timeslots is invisible to the builder.

Play days and the date span don't match. Is that wrong?

No — they're different measures. The span is first game date to last; play days counts the days that actually have games. A Friday–Sunday event that doesn't play Saturday shows a three-day span and two play days. Both numbers are true.

One day has far more games than the others.

Usually the light days are short on timeslots rather than the heavy day being over-booked — the builder puts games where slots exist. Open Assign Timeslots for the age groups involved and compare the days.

Is this the same as QA?

No, and they're complementary. This page reports what's missing — teams and divisions with no games. QA reports what clashes — a team booked twice, a field double-used, a broken bracket. Run both before you publish.

Tip: check this page immediately after every build, before you look at any grid. Two highlighted tiles here take ten seconds to read and will save you finding the same gap by eye an hour later.
