Schedule Dashboard

A one-screen read on the schedule you've built: how big it is, how it spreads across the days, and — the part worth looking at — what got left out. Open it by clicking the stat band under the title on the Scheduling Checklist; the arrow beside the title brings you back.

It fills in after the build.

Before there are games there is nothing to summarize — readiness is the only status that matters, and the Scheduling Checklist is where that lives.

The tiles

Games
Total games on the books for the event.
Play days
Distinct days with games, plus the first-to-last date span beneath. A weekend event with a gap shows both numbers honestly.
Fields in use
How many fields the build actually put games on — which can be fewer than you assigned.
Divisions scheduled
Shown as scheduled / schedulable when any division came up short, with the shortfall named underneath.
Teams scheduled
Shown as scheduled / active when any active team has no game at all.
A highlighted tile means something was skipped.

"3 divisions without games" or "12 teams without a game" is the build telling you it couldn't place them — usually missing timeslots on a day, teams still in Unassigned, or not enough field capacity. A build that skips work does not fail; it just builds less. These two tiles are how you notice.

Games per day

A bar per play day, so you can see the shape of the event at a glance. A day carrying far more games than the others usually means the other days are short on timeslots — worth a look in Assign Timeslots before anyone tries to play it.

Where to act on what you see

  • Teams or divisions without games — check pools and timeslots on the Scheduling Checklist, then re-build on the Schedule hub.
  • Fewer fields in use than expected — a field with no timeslots for a day gets no games, even though it's assigned to the event.
  • Conflicts, not counts — that's QA, not this screen. This one tells you what's missing; QA tells you what clashes.
