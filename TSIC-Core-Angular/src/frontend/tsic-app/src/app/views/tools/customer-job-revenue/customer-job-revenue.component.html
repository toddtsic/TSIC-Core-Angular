An event is included when it is active between your start and end dates. Everything else is left out, so you see the events you asked about rather than your whole history.
Each included event then shows its full record, as of the end date. A team that registered and paid long before your start date is still counted — your start date chooses events, it does not hide their money.
Each month shows what happened in that month. A team charged a deposit in March that pays its balance in July appears in both: the charge under March, the money under July. Players on a payment plan appear in every month they were drafted. So Billed and Collected are not expected to match inside any one month — only the totals tie out.
Discounts, Corrections and Refunds are extra detail, not extra money. Discounts are fee reductions you granted — Billed is already net of them, so they are never part of Collected. Corrections and Refunds are the opposite: they are already inside Collected, shown separately so you can see them. Do not add these three to Collected — you would be counting the same money twice.
Corrections are mostly money coming in, not comps. A correction is usually you recording a payment that arrived outside the system; a negative one is a write-off. The column is the net of both.
Owed, the last column, is each team's balance as of the end date, shown against the month that team registered. It is the bottom line, not the arithmetic of the row it sits on.
The number in parentheses after a team name is its roster count — how many active players are on that team. }
