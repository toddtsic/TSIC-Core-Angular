Attach national ranking numbers to the teams registered in your event, so you can build pools by strength instead of by guesswork. Whatever you save here shows up as Nat'l Rank over in Pool Assignment.

  1. 1 Start with your own age group. Pick it first and you will see straight away how many of its teams already carry a national ranking, and which ones — open that line to check them before you change anything.
  2. 2 Then pick a season and the national group to match against. The published groups are by graduation class — "Girls 2029" is the class of 2029 — so choose the one holding the same players as your age group. Seeding a new event? Use the most recently completed season; it has a full year of results behind it.
  3. 3 Review every match, not just the weak ones. Teams are paired by comparing club name, colour, graduation year and state — it is a best guess, and clubs rename teams between seasons. Anything left over appears on the right; use Match on a team to pair it by hand, or Change to correct one the matcher got wrong. If a team is misnamed in your event, the pencil renames it here only — and we re-check that one team against the rankings still unmatched, which often finds the pairing the old name was hiding.
  4. 4 Save at a confidence level you trust. 75%+ is the safe default; anything you paired by hand is always saved, and anything you removed is cleared. Matches below the level you pick are left exactly as they are. Then sort by Nat'l Rank in Pool Assignment and snake the top teams across pools so the strong ones do not stack up in one bracket. Re-run this any time — saving again just overwrites.
