Attach national ranking numbers to the teams registered in your event, so you can build pools by strength instead of by guesswork. Whatever you save here shows up as Nat'l Rank over in Pool Assignment.

  1. 1 Pick a season, then two age groups. The published groups are by graduation class — "Girls 2029" is the class of 2029 — so pair each one with the age group in your event that holds those players. Seeding a new event? Use the most recently completed season; it has a full year of results behind it.
  2. 2 Review every match, not just the weak ones. Teams are paired by comparing club name, colour, graduation year and state — it is a best guess, and clubs rename teams between seasons. Anything left over appears on the right; use Match on a team to pair it by hand, or Change to correct one the matcher got wrong.
  3. 3 Save at a confidence level you trust. 75%+ is the safe default; anything you paired by hand is always saved. Then sort by Nat'l Rank in Pool Assignment and snake the top teams across pools so the strong ones do not stack up in one bracket. Re-run this any time — saving again just overwrites.

How It Works
AI Matching
AI discovers published national rankings on the internet, then compares club name, team color, graduation year, and state against your registered teams.
75%+ 50–74%
Manual Override
Match — pair an unmatched team via the sidebar. Change — reassign an AI match.
Save
Choose a confidence threshold. Manual pairings always included. Rankings appear as Nat'l Rank in Pool Assignment.
Seasons
usclublax.com publishes rankings one season at a time. The Season list mirrors what they currently offer and defaults to the newest — switch it to seed from a completed season instead. The available age groups differ from season to season.
Experimental
This tool reads a third-party website we do not control. If they change their page layout, rename an age group, or go offline, results here can change or stop working without notice. Treat it as an aid to seeding, not a source of record — use at your own discretion.
