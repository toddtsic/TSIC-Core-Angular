New registrations per day, dated by when each was created, counted only if something has been paid on it. The dollars are what has been paid on those registrations as of now — not the money that arrived that day: a registration made on the 5th and paid on the 10th sits on the 5th with its payment, so recent days can still grow. A club rep is counted once, on the day the rep registered for the event, carrying the paid total across every team that rep has entered since. Admin registrations are left out — provisioning, not intake. The chart follows the Event and Role dropdowns and draws every day, a day with nothing paid as zero; the table always covers the whole scope and lists only days that took a paid registration.
