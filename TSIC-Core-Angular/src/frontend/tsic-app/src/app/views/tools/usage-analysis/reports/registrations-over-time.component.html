What came IN per bucket, counted from each row's own creation stamp — not who used the site. Players and club reps are registrations; teams are teams, so the People column adds the two registration columns and leaves Teams out. Teams is not the club rep count: a rep registers once per event and then adds teams under that one registration, so a day can bring thirty teams and no new reps. Unlike the usage reports these numbers add up — a registration is created once, so a bucket's count never counts it again and the tiles are span totals. Counted only if it is still standing: a registration activates at payment (or at completion of a free event), so carts started and abandoned are not here. Admin and Scorer registrations are left out entirely — provisioning rather than intake. The newest bucket is the current one, still filling.
