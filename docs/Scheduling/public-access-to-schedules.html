Public Access To Schedules

Turning on Allow Public Schedule Access makes your game schedule readable by anyone with the link — no login required. What it does not do is announce it. Nothing appears on your event page by itself.

Instead, saving that setting automatically creates a bulletin for you, titled Schedules Available!, in Communications › Bulletins. That bulletin is created Inactive — it is written and waiting, but invisible to everyone until you set it Active yourself. You decide when the announcement goes up, and you can edit or rename it before it does. If you had an older schedule bulletin from a previous season, it is removed at the same time so you are not left with two.

That gap between access and announcement is deliberate: it gives you a window to have your club reps check the schedule before the general public sees a word about it.
